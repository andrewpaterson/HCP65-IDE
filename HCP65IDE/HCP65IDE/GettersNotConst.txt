with the first error (its file and line as they were then).  b doesn't change what a getter returns, so a getter that
hands out a writable pointer or reference into its object stays as it is: they are yours to decide.

    non-const function returns (most often CChars::Text's char*), C2440 and C2663.
  - Calls something of its object's that isn't const: another function that changes the object, or one that
    hands out a pointer (an array's Get(i), a list's GetHead), C2662.
  - Other: anything else the compiler said.
