Cleaning up the IDE's own code (plan, 2026-11-28)
=================================================

The user (2026-11-28): "Look through the HCP65-IDE for code that could be better, repetition, smells, large classes,
long functions.  Anything that is inelegant.", then "Plan 2, 3 and 5 and make sure they really are mechanical.  Record
the rest".  The review found seven things; 2, 3 and 5 are planned here and the rest are recorded at the end.
Nothing here is a go-ahead: each step is built only when the user says so, and the questions at the end are the
user's, asked one at a time.

This is about the IDE's code, not the refactorings it offers (those are Refactorings.txt).

Paths are in HCP65-IDE/HCP65IDE/HCP65IDE unless they say otherwise.  Line numbers are as of 65471cf.


What "mechanical" means here, and how it was checked
-----------------------------------------------------

A step is mechanical if every edit in it is a move of code unchanged, or one fixed substitution made at every site,
and no function's logic is rewritten.  Each was checked by comparing the code itself, not by reading a sample:

  - "Identical" bodies were compared whole, with only the class's name and the white space evened out.
  - "Stands alone" was checked by listing every field and member function a body uses, and what those use in turn.
  - The loops were matched by exact patterns on all three parts of the for; a loop that differs at all is not counted.

Where the check found that something is not mechanical, or was not the problem the review took it for, it is out of
the plan and says why under "Checked and left out".


The steps
---------

Each step builds and passes the checks by itself, and is one commit.  The order is the safest first.  Every step
is mechanical but B4, which says so.

T. Text helpers (the review's 5)

T1. One home for the text helpers that exist.  They are declared in whichever feature first needed them:
    IsIDENameChar and IsIDEIdentifier in IDERename.h (28 other files use the first), FindIDETextOffset,
    FindIDENextLine and FindIDELineStart in IDESafeDelete.h (20 other files use the first, 289 times),
    FindIDEWordAt in IDEFind.h, FindIDECommentStart in IDEMoveMembers.h, FindIDETextPosition in
    IDELocationPreview.h, and SkipIDESpacesBack is static in IDECompletionContext.cpp:205.  IDECodeModel.cpp:2738
    declares IsIDENameChar itself to avoid including IDERename.h.
    They move, unchanged, to a new IDEText.h and IDEText.cpp (items of HCP65IDE.vcxproj and its .filters).  The
    headers they leave include IDEText.h, so no other file changes.
    With them: FindIDELineColumn, which is static and written twice (IDEConvertMacro.cpp:420,
    IDEMoveNamespace.cpp:360).  The two differ by one test: the second stops at the text's end.  The second is kept;
    for a place inside the text they are the same.

T2. The scanning loops that are one of the helpers.  Of the 301 for loops with an empty body, 141 are exactly one of
    nine forms, in 27 files:

      forwards over spaces and tabs                       43   SkipIDEBlanks (new)
      back to the line's start                            32   FindIDELineStart (exists)
      forwards to the line's end                          18   FindIDELineEnd (new)
      back over spaces and tabs                           12   SkipIDEBlanksBack (new)
      forwards over a name                                 9   FindIDENameEnd (new)
      back over a name                                     8   FindIDENameStart (new)
      forwards over spaces, tabs, returns and new lines    7   SkipIDEWhiteSpace (new)
      back over spaces, tabs, returns and new lines        6   SkipIDEWhiteSpaceBack (SkipIDESpacesBack, renamed)
      forwards over spaces, tabs and returns               6   SkipIDEBlanksAndReturns (new; question 3)

    Each "for (x = from; test; x++) { }" becomes "x = Helper(szText, from);": three lines to one.  A script makes
    the edits from the same patterns that counted them, prints each, and must report 141; its diff is read before
    it is built.  The new helpers are three lines each, written as FindIDELineStart is.

    The patterns, exactly, so the count can be made again.  A loop counts if it is a "for (start; test; step)" on
    one line in one of this folder's .cpp files, with "{" alone on the next line and "}" alone on the one after;
    its step is "x++" (forwards) or "x--" (back) for one variable x; its test is one of these to the letter, T
    being one expression for the text throughout (szText, szText.Text(), psSite->szText.Text()):
      forwards over spaces and tabs      (T[x] == ' ') || (T[x] == '\t')
      ... and returns                    (T[x] == ' ') || (T[x] == '\t') || (T[x] == '\r')
      ... and new lines                  (T[x] == ' ') || (T[x] == '\t') || (T[x] == '\r') || (T[x] == '\n')
      to the line's end                  (T[x] != '\0') && (T[x] != '\n')
      over a name                        IsIDENameChar(T[x])
      back to the line's start           (x > 0) && (T[x - 1] != '\n')
      back over spaces and tabs          (x > 0) && ((T[x - 1] == ' ') || (T[x - 1] == '\t'))
      ... returns and new lines          (x > 0) && ((T[x - 1] == ' ') || (T[x - 1] == '\t') || (T[x - 1] == '\r')
                                           || (T[x - 1] == '\n'))
      back over a name                   (x > 0) && IsIDENameChar(T[x - 1])
    and its start is one of four: "x = from" (105 of the 141; the call is given from), nothing (27; given x),
    "x++" (7; given x + 1) or "x--" (2; given x - 1).  A test in another order, with another bound than 0, or on
    a pointer (*sz) doesn't count.

T3. The word at the caret.  IDETemplateParameter.cpp:426 and :1916, IDEConvertMacro.cpp:289 and
    IDEContextActions.cpp:565 each find the name the caret is in or just after with their own loops.
    FindIDEWordAt (IDEFind.cpp:910) gives the same start and end in every case, with no name too.  Four sites.

M. Things that are in the wrong class (the review's 3)

M1. Where a file's text comes from.  CIDEFileTexts (IDECodeModel.h:178) is "the editor's text if the file is open,
    else the disk's".  CIDEFindInFiles implements it (IDEFindInFiles.cpp:904), and its body only asks
    CIDEDocuments for the file's tab.  So every refactoring that parses a file names Find in Files to do it.
    CIDEDocuments implements it instead; the body moves unchanged, with its two limits
    (IDE_FIND_FILE_MAXIMUM, IDE_FIND_BINARY_CHECK).  Sites, each one substitution:
      69  "&mpcContent->GetFindInFiles()" passed as the texts  ->  "&mpcContent->GetDocuments()"
      40  "GetFindInFiles()->GetFileText("                     ->  "GetDocuments()->GetFileText("
       3  Find in Files' own calls of GetFileText              ->  through the documents
    The other 38 uses of GetFindInFiles() are Find in Files itself and stay.

M2. CIDEFileEdits (the edits to several files, applied as one undo) is declared in IDEDefinitions.h:59 and defined
    from IDEDefinitions.cpp:96 (about 220 lines), though 33 files use it.  It uses nothing of CIDEDefinitions.  It
    moves, unchanged, beside CIDETextEdits in IDERefactorUnit.h and .cpp (no new project file).  Of the 23 files that
    include IDEDefinitions.h, 19 use nothing else of it; they include IDERefactorUnit.h instead.  IDEDefinitions.h
    also brings in IDEChangeSignature.h, so the compiler says which of the 19 need that named too.

M3. Problems Detected.  CIDEChangeSignature::ShowConflicts (IDEChangeSignature.cpp:509) is the dialog 13 other
    refactorings show before they change anything.  It uses only the content and its own dialog pointer.  It moves
    to a class of its own, CIDEConflicts, reached through the content as the features are; 14 call sites change
    name, and refactoring.md's row for it.  The least of the steps; the user decided it is done (question 1).

B. A base class for the features (the review's 2)

B1. CIDEFeature.  71 classes derive from CObject and hold "CIDEContent* mpcContent"; each also declares and
    defines Save and Load that return false, and starts Class() with U_Pointer(mpcContent).  A new CIDEFeature
    (IDEFeature.h and .cpp, built as CIDEResultsSearch is, the base the results searches already share) has the
    pointer, the two stubs and the registration.  In each of the 71: the base's name changes, the field, the two
    declarations and the two definitions go, and Class()'s first line becomes CIDEFeature::Class().  Init is left
    as it is.  About six lines a class.
    Left as they are: the four settings pages (CSettingsPage is their base), CIDEEditCommands (CEditCommands is),
    and the four that are not objects (CIDEFileEdits, CIDELightTyper, CIDEExpectedTypes, CIDEExpressionTyper).
    CIDEScript stays a friend of the classes it is a friend of; a friend reaches what its class inherits.

B2. "Is there a symbol at the caret."  Ten functions have the same body, three locals and one line
    (CIDEConvertPointer::CanConvert, CIDEMoveMembers::CanMove, CIDESafeDelete::CanSafeDelete and seven more), and
    five more are "is there an active tab".  CIDEFeature gets IsSymbolAtCaret and HasActiveTab, and the fifteen
    bodies become one call.  The functions keep their names: commands name them as their enabled checks.

B3. The parsed files a refactoring keeps while it works.  GetUnit is the same 16 lines in seven classes
    (CIDEConvertPointer, CIDEDefinitions, CIDEMoveMembers, CIDESafeDelete, CIDEMoveNamespace, CIDEChangeSignature,
    CIDEMoveClass), KillUnits the same nine in six, and each has the array, three of them a typedef of their own
    for it.  Each touches the array in the same ten places.
    A class CIDERefactorUnits, beside CIDERefactorUnit in IDERefactorUnit.h and .cpp (no new project file), has the
    array and what the copies do: Init with the content, Kill, Get (GetUnit's body) and Clear (KillUnits').  It is a
    member, not a base (question 2): the seven have a cache, they aren't a kind of class.  In each of the seven:
      - the field becomes "CIDERefactorUnits mcUnits", and its line in Init, Class and Free changes with it (in
        Free, "KillUnits(); mapcUnits.Kill();" is "mcUnits.Kill();", which does both);
      - GetUnit, KillUnits and the typedef go;
      - "GetUnit(" becomes "mcUnits.Get(" and "KillUnits()" becomes "mcUnits.Clear()": 54 calls, by script, which
        must report 54 (32 and 22).  In the five classes left with no GetUnit a missed call doesn't compile.
    Two keep functions of those names, each one line, public as they are now, for their 11 callers in other classes:
    CIDEMoveNamespace (GetUnit, KillUnits) and CIDEMoveMembers (GetUnit).  CIDEMoveMembers::KillUnits stays whole
    until B4, as it empties a second array too, so its eight calls inside CIDEMoveMembers stay as they are.
    B3 doesn't need B1: it can be built before it.

B4. Move Members' second cache.  Not mechanical: the one step with new logic in it, and so apart.
    CIDEMoveMembers::GetCheckedUnit (IDEMoveMembers.cpp:634) is an eighth copy of GetUnit over a second array,
    mapcChecked.  It differs in two places: it parses with the check, and a file counts only if IsChecked.
    CIDERefactorUnits is given that as a flag at Init (parsed, or parsed and checked), and CIDEMoveMembers holds a
    second one, mcChecked.  GetCheckedUnit goes (it has one call, :670) and KillUnits becomes two Clears.
    Before it is built the checks that reach GetCheckedUnit are found (a line printed there and a run of the
    checks), so it is known which ones prove the step; if none do, one is written first.


How each step is checked
------------------------

  - Before T1: a Release build of HCP65IDE and HCP65IDERef in a build folder of this work's own, and the whole of
    IDEChecks.py, kept as the baseline.  A check that fails before the work is named then, not found later.
  - After each step: the same build and the whole of IDEChecks.py, against the baseline.  Nothing a user sees
    changes in any step, so any difference is a fault in the step.
  - T2: the script's count and its diff, before the build.
  - At the end: the new files are in HCP65IDE.vcxproj and its .filters (edited as bytes, keeping the BOM and the
    line endings they have: LF on 2026-11-28), and the solution builds with MSBuild; the pages' references to code
    resolve (hcp65index -resolve, in the capped scope); architecture.md says what CIDEFeature is, and
    refactoring.md what CIDERefactorUnits is and where Problems Detected is.


Checked and left out
--------------------

  - The Queue functions (52 in 36 files: "QueueMove posts ShowMove", so a menu's popup has gone before the dialog
    shows).  They are alike but not removable from the IDE's side: each is a command's target.  Taking them away
    needs a command that posts itself (a flag on CCommand, in WindowLib).  That is a change to CommonLibraries and
    a design, not a substitution.  Recorded below (R5).
  - CIDEChangeSignature::ReadSite and FindSignature, which the review called misplaced because 15 and 6 calls come
    from other refactorings.  They are not: FindSignature and the ten functions under it (ReadSite one of them) are
    the model of a function's signature, its declarations and its calls, and the other refactorings use it as
    that.  The review was wrong about these two.  Splitting the model from the dialog and the change would be a
    real piece of design; recorded (R4).
  - IsIDEIncludeLine, static in IDEFileRename.cpp:459 and IDEMoveClass.cpp:128: the same name for two different
    questions with different parameters.  Not a copy.  One could be renamed.
  - The other 160 empty loops match none of the nine forms: they look for a bracket, stop at a bound that isn't
    0, climb a syntax node's parents.  Two of them skip spaces, tabs and returns as the ninth form does, but walk a
    pointer, not an index (IDEExpressionTyper.cpp:259, IDESafeDelete.cpp:1335); the helpers take the text and a
    place in it, so those two stay.
  - The libraries' loops (62 more: IndexLib 32, TemplateLib 17, VisualStudioLib 13): IDEText.h is the IDE's, and
    they can't include it.


The rest of the review, recorded (not planned)
----------------------------------------------

R1. The script runner (IDEScript.cpp).  RunEditStep (:1920) is 1,351 lines with 178 branches, RunViewStep (:3844)
    998, RunCommand (:197) 699.  Steps are matched by prefix lengths counted by hand ("renamefilesearch:", 17) and
    read by index (szStep[17]); there are 303 such compares in the code, all correct on 2026-11-28.  "Save all if
    gbIDEScriptRenameSaves" is written 30 times.  gaszIDEScriptCommands (:45) is a second list of the names, kept
    in step by hand.  What would mend it: a table of name and handler, a function for each step, the table being
    the list.  The biggest single gain, and it touches every check.

R2. Refactorings that are one function.  38 functions are over 200 lines and 148 over 100.
    CIDETemplateParameter::RemoveParameter (IDETemplateParameter.cpp:1844) is 486 lines with 56 locals: five phases
    joined by "if (bResult)", then the Kill calls.  CIDEExtractClass::Extract (IDEExtractClass.cpp:595) is 412
    lines with 50 locals.  What would mend one: a function for each phase, the locals in a struct with Init and
    Kill as the SIDE... structs are.  Each is its own piece of work, done with the function's checks beside it.

R3. The checks' settings in the program's start.  CIDEApp::Setup (IDEApp.cpp:280) is 343 lines, mostly getenv
    tests.  65 HCP65_IDE_* variables are read in 12 files, 13 of them in more than one.  The features call
    CIDEScript::WatchDialog and StopWatchingDialog 74 times.  What would mend it: the environment read once into
    one struct, and dialogs watched from one place.

R4. Change Signature's file (IDEChangeSignature.cpp, 3,255 lines) holds the signature's model, which twenty other
    refactorings use, and the refactoring itself.  They could be two classes.

R5. Commands that post themselves, so the 52 Queue functions can go (see "Checked and left out").

R6. Smaller repetition:
      - AddFormRow is in three dialogs (IDEBuildConfigurationsDialog.cpp:691, IDERunConfigurationsDialog.cpp:326,
        IDEChangeSignatureDialog.cpp:234) and CreateLabel in four settings pages; their comments say "as CDialog's
        own rows are", so CDialog could have them.
      - The scope list is filled the same way in IDEDuplicates.cpp:305 and IDEUsages.cpp:1195.
      - VisualStudioLib/VcxProject.cpp: RemoveIncludes (:1407), AddIncludesBeside (:1731) and RenameIncludes (:1862)
        each scan for the Include=" element the same way (:1456 and :1780 are the same for 19 lines), and
        RenameItem, AddItemBeside and RemoveItem again.
      - BuildLib: CBuildConfiguration (BuildConfiguration.h:123) and CBuildPlain (BuildPlain.h:83) hold the same ten
        flags, two named the opposite way round (mbMathTables and mbNoMathTables, mbWarnMissingOverride and
        mbNoOverrideWarning).  CBuildPlain::ParseOption (BuildPlain.cpp:211) is one 286 line chain.
      - GitLib: CGitRepository::IsTracked builds by hand what RunOnPath does.
      - Checks/IDEChecks.py is 6,156 lines, 5,200 of them one list of 554 checks; 154 steps wait a fixed time
        (wait:1500 is 59 of them).

R7. Left on purpose.  CIDEContent has 118 functions and about 70 features, and a new feature is written in five
    places (the field, Class, Setup, the getter's declaration and its definition).  One class per feature behind the
    content's getters is the user's design of 2026-11-06 (architecture.md).
