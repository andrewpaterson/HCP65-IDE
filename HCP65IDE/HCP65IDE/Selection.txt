Selection in the HCP65 IDE's editor (plan, 2026-11-12)
=====================================================

Chosen by the user: Extend/Shrink Selection, column (box) selection, and the mouse and look extras.  Not chosen:
multiple carets.

Today: CTextEditing (BaseLib) has one caret and one anchor, the selection between them.  Keys select by character,
word, home/end, page and document; Control+A; Control+W selects the word at the caret (CIC_SelectWholeWord).  The
pointer drags to select and shift+press extends.  There is no double click on text, no triple click, no gutter
selection, no drag and drop, no occurrence highlighting.  CTextArea draws the selection as a range of shapes in its
selection map (UpdateMarks).  CCodeEditor's gutter is a CTextArea that calls a gutter click event with the line.


Step 1 - Clicks (WindowLib CTextEditingInput, shared by CTextArea and CTextField)
  Double click selects the word (CTextEditing::SelectWholeWord's rules); triple click selects the line with its line
  feed (a field: all of it).  Dragging on after a double or triple click extends by whole words or whole lines, keeping
  the first word or line selected, as VS and IntelliJ do.  Counted with SDoubleClick's time and distance.
  Tests: TestTextEditingInput.

  Step 1 in detail:
  How a press arrives now: button 1 unmodified -> CIC_ActivateComponent (CTextEditingInput::ActivateComponent moves
  the caret to the nearest gap, then CFocus::StartDrag(FD_Select) -> StartDragSelect anchors there); the release ->
  CIC_ExecuteComponent; moves -> ContDragSelect; Shift/Control+button 1 -> CIC_StartDragSelect directly (extends).
  1a. WindowLib SClickCount (in DoubleClick.h/.cpp beside SDoubleClick, sharing DOUBLE_CLICK_MILLISECONDS/PIXELS, now in
      DoubleClick.h): Pressed(x, y) returns 1, 2 or 3 - a press within 500 ms and 4 px of the last press counts on
      from it; a fourth counts as 1 again.  Pressed(x, y, iMilliseconds) for tests.  Forget() on focus lost.
  1b. BaseLib CTextEditing:
      - FindCharacterAt(x, y, &uiPosition): the character under the pointer, not the nearest gap (pointer on the
        right half of 'b' is 'b', not the gap after it); past the line's end it is the line's end.
      - CTextNavigation::FindWordAround(uiPosition, &uiStart, &uiEnd) (built in CTextNavigation, beside the word
        moves; CTextEditing::GetNavigation gives it to the input): CTextNavigation's word rules - a run of word characters, a run
        of spaces/tabs, or one other character (so "->" is two clicks' worth: '-' or '>').  At a line end: nothing
        (start = end).
      - CTextNavigation::FindLineAround(uiPosition, &uiStart, &uiEnd): the line with its line feed (CR LF is one character); the last
        line to the text's end.  Lines, not wrapped rows.  Single line: the whole text.
      Ctrl+W's SelectWholeWord is left as it is (caret based) - step 5 replaces it.
  1c. CTextEditingInput: ActivateComponent counts the press.  1: as now.  2: selects FindWordAround of the character
      under the pointer; 3: FindLineAround.  The selected range is kept as the drag's origin with a unit (character,
      word, line), and StartDragSelect doesn't re-anchor for a word or line press.  ContDragSelect by word or line:
      pointer before the origin -> anchor at the origin's end, caret at the start of the word/line under the pointer;
      after it -> anchor at the origin's start, caret at that word's/line's end; inside -> the origin.  Release ends
      the unit; a Shift+press after it extends by characters from the anchor, as now.  The press's x, y are the
      component's, as the release's.
  1d. Applies to CTextArea, CTextField and so the IDE's editors, Build/Find text areas, Go to dialog field.  The gutter
      is step 2.  CTextArea's read-only text that can't be selected (SetPointerEnabled(false)) gets nothing.
  Tests:
    TestTextEditing (BaseLib, also the 65C64/816/size16 builds): FindCharacterAt left/right half, past line end;
      FindWordAround on a word's start/middle/end char, spaces, tabs, punctuation, UTF-8 letters, line end, empty
      text; FindLineAround first/middle/last line, CR LF, empty line, single line.
    TestTextEditingInput: double press selects the word; triple the line with its LF; fourth is a plain click; slow
      second press (601 ms) and moved one (5 px) are plain; drag after a double left and right by words keeps the
      first word; drag after a triple by lines; Shift+press after a double extends by characters; single line triple
      selects all; SetPointerEnabled(false) ignores them.  SClickCount on its own.
    TestTextArea: double click on a proportional font's character's right half selects its word; TestTextField: double
      and triple click.  Mutation checks: break each rule once and see a test fail.
  IDE: script step doubleclick:/tripleclick: (line, column) + IDEChecks entry; check by hand in Xephyr if you want it.
  Commit: commonlibraries (BaseLib + WindowLib + tests), HCP65-IDE (script step + checks + this file).
  BUILT 2026-11-12.  Tests: TestBaseLib 64/size16, TestBaseLib65 TestTextNavigation+TestTextEditing on the 65C64 and
  the 816, TestWindowLib 32/64; IDE check editor-clicks; 9 mutations each caught.  The TestTextField and TestTextArea
  cases needed a press away from the test's earlier ones first: quick presses within 4 px now count as a double click.

Step 2 - Gutter selection (WindowLib CCodeEditor)
  Pressing a line number selects the line; dragging in the gutter selects whole lines; shift+press extends by lines.
  Glyph and change bar columns keep their clicks.  Tests: TestCodeEditor.

  Step 2 in detail:
  How a gutter press arrives now: the gutter is a CTextArea that can't be selected or focussed, so a press focusses
  the CCodeEditor and its CIC_ActivateComponent action (ClickAction) gives the focus to the code's text area, then
  calls one event by the gap nearest the pointer: glyph column (gap 0) -> glyph click event (IDE: breakpoints), at or
  past the gap after the number on a line with a change bar -> change bar click event (IDE: git's old lines), anything
  else -> gutter click event (CLineZones: toggles a line's listing pages).  Shift/Control+press goes to the focussed
  CCodeEditor as CIC_StartDragSelect, which it has no action for: nothing happens today.
  The gutter's columns: [glyph] mark digits space(change bar).
  2a. Columns by the character under the pointer (CTextArea::FindCharacterAt, passing step 1's CTextEditing one), not
      the nearest gap: the glyph column is the glyph's whole cell, not its left half.  Glyph -> glyph event as now;
      change bar character on a line with a bar -> change bar event as now; the mark character -> gutter click event
      as now (so CLineZones' '+' marks keep working and don't select); the digits, and the bar column on a line with
      no bar -> line selection (below), no event.  Past the gutter's row (a line component's gap) -> the line above,
      as now.
  2b. WindowLib CTextEditingInput::StartLineDrag(x, y, bExtend) (CTextArea passes it on), x, y the text area's:
      - press (bExtend false): the line under y (FindLineAround, with its line feed) is selected and kept as the
        drag's origin, unit TEDU_Line; caret after its line feed, as a triple click leaves it.
      - Shift/Control press (bExtend true): the origin is the anchor alone (the caret when nothing is selected), unit
        TEDU_Line, so the anchor stays where it is, mid-line or not, and the caret goes to the start of the line under
        the pointer (above the anchor) or after its line feed (below).  Needs CTextEditing::GetAnchorPosition.
      - Both start the focus's FD_Select drag on the text area with the unit kept (mbUnitPress), so the moves after it
        are step 1's DragByUnit: dragging up or down the gutter, or on into the code, grows by whole lines.  The click
        count is forgotten so a press in the code after it is a single click.
      CCodeEditor: ClickAction calls it for the number columns; a new CIC_StartDragSelect action does the same with
      bExtend (giving the text area the focus first; the drag that came to the code editor is stopped and the text
      area's started in its place).  Presses outside the gutter are the code's own, as now.
  2c. A text area that can't be selected (SetSelectable(false)) gets focus only, as now.  Double and triple clicks in
      the gutter are just presses (each selects its line); clicks aren't counted there.
  Tests (TestCodeEditor): press on a number selects the line with its LF, caret on the next line; last line to the
    text's end; one-line text; drag down two lines; drag up past the origin keeps the origin's line; drag on into the
    code; Shift+press below and above a mid-line anchor and with no selection; Control+press as Shift; press in a line
    component's gap; glyph cell's right half -> glyph event, no selection; change bar -> bar event; mark column ->
    gutter event, no selection; bar column on a line without a bar selects; not selectable -> nothing; a press in the
    code after a gutter press is a single click.  The existing gutter tests' press points are checked against 2a.
    Mutation checks as step 1.
  IDE: script steps gutterclick:<line> and shiftgutterclick:<line> (press on the line's number, through the window) +
    IDEChecks entry editor-gutter-select (printselection after each).
  Commit: commonlibraries (BaseLib GetAnchorPosition + WindowLib + tests), HCP65-IDE (script steps + check + this file).
  BUILT 2026-11-12 as planned (commonlibraries 755b535c3).  Tests: TestCodeEditorLineSelection, TestWindowLib 32/64,
  TestBaseLib 64/size16; IDE check editor-gutter-select.  8 mutations caught; forgetting the click count in
  StartLineDrag wasn't needed (the press focusses the code editor first, which forgets them), so it was left out.

Step 3 - Drag and drop (CTextEditingInput)
  A press in the selection that moves past the drag distance drags the text: a drop caret follows the pointer, release
  moves the text there (Control: copies) as one undo step; dropping inside the selection does nothing; release without
  a drag puts the caret.  Escape cancels.  Not between components or windows.

Step 4 - Selected text occurrences (CTextArea, opt-in: SetHighlightOccurrences)
  A selection on one line that isn't all spaces has its other exact matches in the document drawn with a quieter
  selection colour (theme TC_TextOccurrence), case-sensitive, up to 1000; found again after selection changes and edits
  (on the tick, not per key).  The IDE turns it on for editors.

Step 4b - Usages of the symbol at the caret (IDE, user's choice: as IntelliJ)
  With no selection, once the caret has rested (~300 ms, the edits delay's clock) on an identifier the index knows,
  its usages in this file (CCPPIndex, as Find Usages finds them) are drawn in the occurrence colour, the declaration
  in a write colour.  Gone when the caret leaves the identifier or the file is edited (until the index catches up
  after the edits pause).  Only symbols the index has: member calls through objects only as far as the semantic
  pass types them.  WindowLib side: CTextArea::SetOccurrences(ranges) used by both 4 and 4b.

Step 5 - Extend/Shrink Selection engine (BaseLib CTextEditing)
  ExtendSelection grows to the smallest range around the selection that is bigger than it; ShrinkSelection goes back
  through the ranges extended through (forgotten when the selection changes any other way).  Text ranges, for any
  text: word, inside quotes, with quotes, inside brackets ( [ { <none>, with brackets, line without its indent, whole
  lines, comment, whole text.  A view or owner can add ranges (CTextSelectionRanges interface) - the IDE's syntax.
  Control+W becomes CIC_ExtendSelection (its first step is the word, as now), Control+Shift+W CIC_ShrinkSelection.
  Tests: TestTextEditing (65C64 + 816 + size16 as BaseLib's are).

Step 6 - Syntax ranges in the IDE
  The parse the IDE already makes for colours (open + after the 400 ms pause, CIDECodeModel edits thread) also
  collects each syntax node's byte range in the file (CCPPSyntax miFirstToken..miLastToken, parent chain) into a
  sorted array kept on the tab with the change count it was parsed at, like the highlights.  Extend uses them while the
  change count matches; when it doesn't (user's choice: parse on demand) Control+W parses the file then and there, on
  the UI thread, as the edits thread would, and uses that parse's ranges (the status bar says "Parsing..." first).
  Headers: only what the includer's parse reached (lazy declarations aren't parsed) - text ranges cover the rest, and
  the text ranges are mixed in everywhere (inside a string or comment the parser has no nodes).
  Checks: IDEChecks script step extend:/shrink.

Step 7 - Box selection engine (BaseLib CTextEditing)
  A box is the anchor's and caret's lines with an x from each (pixels from the view, so tabs and spans are right).
  Each line's range is its characters between the two x's; lines shorter than the left edge have an empty range at
  their end.  Typing, backspace, delete, tab, paste and cut act on every line's range in one undo step
  (ReplaceRanges); a zero-width box is a caret on every line.  Copy gives the lines joined by line feeds; pasting n
  lines into an n-line box puts one on each line, other text goes on every line.  Moving without shift, Escape, or
  a click ends the box (a plain caret where the caret was).  Undo puts a plain selection back (box not remembered).
  Tests: TestTextEditing.

Step 8 - Box selection drawing and input (CTextArea, CTextEditingInput, IDE)
  Drawn as one rectangle per row.  Shift+Alt+arrows/Home/End and Shift+Alt+drag make a box (VS's keys); Alt+drag too
  where the window manager doesn't take it.  Edit menu: Extend Selection, Shrink Selection, Select All; IDE checks.
